# Customer intent must come from the customer

Draft, updated October25, 2026. Current deployed main `d550dc1` is integrated without rebasing; **not merged, deployed or ready**. The earlier base was `5bafe93`.

## Failure and integrated repair

Previously the handler classified the post title, newest message and all clipped earlier replies together. A bot's own incorrect action label, or another participant's request, could therefore select a canned financial/privacy answer and bypass normal technical answering. A planner could also revive an explicitly rejected action through its rewritten goal and retrieval queries.

The existing bounded history fetch now supplies application-assigned roles and local turn IDs, derived from author ID rather than display names. One chronological owner-intent projection is folded before planning-view clipping. Recognized withdrawals remain in force on later identifier-only turns; independent new owner actions reopen their own lanes. Other participants cannot withdraw or establish owner actions. Direct-reply continuations already verify the original customer. Answer/reviewer history is retained; earlier-post context remains excluded from routing. No extra fetch, retention, private tool or model call is introduced.

`requestCorrections.js` recognizes bounded English/Spanish first-person denials of financial/deletion actions, removes that grammatical action signal while retaining subsequent technical facts/positive requests, and reports which interpretation was rejected. An explicit correction can withdraw that historical action route. Merely supplying a technical version does not cancel an earlier real financial request. Current independently recognized positive actions remain protected.

The same projection controls deterministic routing and planner normalization. Thread titles and other speakers may inform non-personal topic retrieval, not owner action authority. Planning uses application-owned actor labels alongside a compact owner/action record. A personal interpretation not already supported by a known owner action must cite application-supplied owner turn IDs in `intent_sources`; absent, malformed or other-speaker references cannot grant that interpretation. A rejected or withdrawn interpretation loses its generated goal, facts, requirements, queries and summary together. Unconfirmed personal intent retains a conservative generic person path rather than treating missing English keywords as proof that no multilingual request exists. Language, data-risk and human-request flags remain.

The owner/action record is also supplied to the answer and reviewer. Relevant technical context remains available for retrieval and full answer/reviewer context is not erased. Two initially failing technical-follow-up regressions exposed an over-narrow retrieval projection and are now repaired without accepting untrusted personal-action context. Real owner financial/privacy/device requests, explicit and ambiguous reopening, mixed actions and the prior memory-off prompt fixtures remain protected.

October25 independent re-review found two more context-loss paths at `936b718`: a valid planner that echoed only a version/caption bypassed the sanitized case context, and withdrawing a financial/privacy action discarded the whole original mixed-content owner turn. The follow-up carries the same bounded sanitized projection alongside the planner's standalone question; it never restores unfiltered routing history. Four observed red regressions pass at `abcd59c`, but additional original controls show that masking a whole routing lane also loses independent financial/deletion requests. The final follow-up therefore reuses the correction recognizer's existing direct-object vocabulary as explicit withdrawal scopes: refund, payment adjustment, account deletion and data deletion. Only a withdrawn scope is masked; surviving owner signals are re-evaluated for routing. Cancelling an order or reporting a duplicate charge is not withdrawn by declining a refund, and declining account deletion does not erase a separate data-deletion request. The shared owner record distinguishes broad lanes from specific withdrawn scopes. Original answer/reviewer history remains intact. This is bounded withdrawal policy, not a new general semantic classifier or clause parser.

Turn provenance proves who supplied text, not that a model's semantic interpretation of it is correct. The grammar recognizer remains bounded English/Spanish; unknown constructions stay conservative. This is not a general multilingual semantic classifier or a replacement for independent generated-answer testing. No earlier-post memory is enabled.
