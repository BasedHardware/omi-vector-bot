## Required coherent pipeline

1. Ingestion must carry trustworthy source declarations into the existing parent-page metadata and formatted evidence. Keep source authority, product family/model, platform, app version and firmware version distinct. Do not infer a universal scope from missing metadata, a title, brand, fetch time or a newer date. The existing source-date preference is not an applicability verdict.
2. Bind those declarations to explicit customer context outside the model. Keep matched, explicitly incompatible, missing customer detail and unknown source scope distinct. Customer descriptions can identify a requested device or version; they cannot verify backend state. A planner or reviewer boolean is a proposal, not proof.
3. An unresolved material applicability condition must survive ordinary-FAQ approval clearing, second look, selection and presentation. Ask one genuinely useful missing detail where that can resolve the scope; otherwise use the existing verified private handoff. Removing email or adding reply-here does not establish that next step.
4. Preserve current human-action intent before redirect removal, without treating a conditional future contact footer as a current requirement. Keep the staff delivery notice application-owned and distinguish delivery from acceptance. A failed delivery retains the existing honest fallback.
5. Contradiction requires more than different strings or dates. Preserve complementary facts and protective warnings. Unknown semantic relationships remain unresolved; do not claim a general solver from matching IDs or model-reported completeness.
